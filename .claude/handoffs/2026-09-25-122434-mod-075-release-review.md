## Codebase Understanding

### Architecture Overview

- The mod reconciles on a 10 s clock: `collect()` reads result.json (4 s pass budget, resumes at `gate.collectFrom`, first worker exempt), then `flagStalls()` probes `agent-tmux status --json` (4 s, longest-unprobed first via `gate.probedAt`). Delivery is `$.prompt.submit`; acks are written only after the session accepts.
- Ack keys per session: `tmux-agent.reported.<sid>`. Episode id `<name>@<since>`; notice-only acks `#launch` (launch-failed) and `#exited` (pane gone) close the notice, not the episode — a late result is still delivered once. `episodeOf()` strips both suffixes; `settled()` = delivered or exited-noticed.
- The wrapper decides quota/login stops (`runtime_blocker_for_text`): last 10 non-empty lines, column-0 error shapes or claude's text right after `⎿`, reset by newer output markers. `⚠` counts only with a quota phrase.
- The `/tmux` panel is the `AbovePrompt` band (terminal only). Every drawn row is counted against `maxRows`; overflow scrolls the band and disarms digit hotkeys.

- Every review finding becomes a ported regression test plus a mutation in the scratchpad runner (`<scratchpad>/mut/run.py` for the mod, `<scratchpad>/wmut/run.py` for the wrapper). A mutation that survives means the test does not test the fix.

- Letter hotkeys press only while the band is focused; digits only from an empty composer.
